Licenses

What is inside the app, and under what terms.

Reel's own code is MIT. The binaries it ships keep their own licenses, and those are listed here rather than buried in a build script.

yt-dlp — Unlicense, with GPLv3 components in the binary

yt-dlp's source is released under the Unlicense. The prebuilt standalone executable that Reel ships is a bundle that also contains third-party code under other licenses, including GPLv3-or-later components. That aggregate is distributed under GPLv3-or-later.

Reel runs the binary as a separate process and does not link against it, but the binary is still redistributed inside the app, so its notices travel with it. The authoritative list: THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. Upstream also offers the source of those components on request through its issue tracker.

ffmpeg — LGPL 2.1+ or GPL, depending on the build

ffmpeg is licensed under the LGPL 2.1-or-later by default. Builds that enable GPL-only components are GPL. The exact terms of the bundled build are printed by the binary itself:

Reel.app/Contents/Resources/ffmpeg -L

Source and license text: ffmpeg.org/legal.html, source downloads.

App icon — ISC
