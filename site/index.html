How it works

Three steps. No setup required.

Reel lives in your menu bar and watches for media links. Adding one takes a single keystroke.

Copy a link

Copy any video link in your browser with ⌘ C. Reel notices it right away.

Press ⌘V in Reel

Switch to Reel and press ⌘ V, or click Add in the menu bar popover. You can also drag links onto the window.

It's on your Mac

Reel downloads the file in your default format and notifies you when it's done.

Features

Small app. Serious engine.

Under the hood is yt-dlp, the most capable downloader there is. Reel makes it feel like a native Mac app.

Clipboard-first

No URL fields to paste into. Reel picks up the link you copied, and you can drag in a whole .txt list.

1,800+ sites

YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, X, Instagram, Reddit, SoundCloud and every other site yt-dlp supports.

Video or audio

Best quality MP4, 1080p for compatibility, MP3, M4A, or the original file untouched.

Playlists

Grab a single video or a whole playlist, with a limit you choose.

Parallel & resumable

Run up to 6 downloads at once, pause and resume them, and pick up where you left off after a restart.

Subtitles & chapters

Embed subtitles (including auto-generated ones), chapters, metadata and thumbnails.

Skip the sponsors

SponsorBlock removes sponsor and self-promo segments automatically.

Self-healing

Reel retries temporary YouTube errors and updates yt-dlp from inside the app, with no restart needed.

Private by design

No accounts, analytics or telemetry. Everything stays on your Mac.

Menu bar

Always one click away.

The popover shows what's downloading and what just finished. Every item gets its thumbnail.

  • Live progress, speed and time remaining
  • Click a finished file to open it
  • Hover to pause, resume or reveal in Finder
Settings

Only what matters.

Choose a save folder and a default format and you're done. The power options wait one tab away.

  • Subtitles, embeds, SponsorBlock and file names
  • Browser login cookies for age-restricted videos
  • Concurrency, low power mode and auto cleanup
Install

Up and running in a minute.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Release builds already include yt-dlp and ffmpeg.

Download the app

Get the latest release from GitHub.

  1. Download Reel.app.zip and unzip it.
  2. Move Reel.app to your Applications folder.
  3. The first time you launch it, right-click → Open.
Download Reel.app.zip

Reel isn't notarized yet. If macOS blocks it, run this once:

$ xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Reel.app

Build from source

Requires Xcode 16 and Homebrew.

$ brew install xcodegen yt-dlp ffmpeg
$ git clone https://github.com/waylake/reel.git
$ cd reel && xcodegen generate
$ open Reel.xcodeproj

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Reel free?

Yes. Reel is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

A download suddenly fails with "403" or "page needs to be reloaded".

YouTube changes often. Open Settings → Advanced → yt-dlp → Update. The new version takes effect right away. Reel also retries temporary errors and updates yt-dlp automatically.

How do I download age-restricted or members-only videos?

In Settings → Downloads → Login cookies, choose the browser you're signed in with (Safari, Chrome or Firefox). Reel passes the cookies to yt-dlp locally. They never leave your Mac.

Where do my files go?

By default they go to ~/Movies/Reel. You can choose any folder in Settings → General.

Does Reel collect any data?

No. Reel has no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. It only checks GitHub for app updates. See the privacy policy.

Is downloading videos legal?

It depends on the content and where you live. Download only what you have the right to download, and respect each site's terms of service.
