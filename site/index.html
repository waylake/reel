01 / How it works

Three steps, one shortcut.

No URL field to paste into. No format dialog to dismiss. Reel works from the link you already copied.

01⌘C

Copy the link

Any video or audio page: YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, TikTok, SoundCloud, and the other 1,800 sites yt-dlp knows.

02⌘V

Paste it in Reel

Reel lives in the menu bar. It reads the clipboard on your Mac, sees the link, and offers it in the popover — ⌘V adds it to the queue. You can also drag a link or a .txt list onto the window.

03~/Movies/Reel

Pick a preset once

Best quality MP4, 1080p H.264, MP3, M4A, or the original file untouched. Every download uses it until you change it. Files land where you point them.

02 / What it does

A small app around a serious engine.

Reel does not implement any extraction itself. yt-dlp does that work, and ffmpeg merges and converts. Reel is the native part: the queue, the clipboard, the cards, the update path.

Clipboard
Reel reads the clipboard locally to spot media links. What it finds shows up in the menu bar; nothing is uploaded. You can turn the detection off in Settings.
Presets
Best quality MP4, 1080p H.264 for compatibility, MP3, M4A, or the original file with no re-encoding.
Playlists
Queue a playlist or an entire channel, with an item limit so a 900-video channel does not fill the disk.
Rich files
Embed subtitles, including auto-generated ones, plus chapters, metadata and thumbnails. Skip sponsor segments with SponsorBlock.
Engine
yt-dlp and ffmpeg ship with the app. When a site changes, update the engine in place — no app restart, no terminal. Transient YouTube errors are retried with backoff.
Sign-in
Members-only or age-restricted video? Pass cookies from Safari, Chrome or Firefox. They stay on your Mac.
Queue
Up to six downloads at once. Quit whenever you like; unfinished items resume on the next launch.
03 / Screens

Menu bar, window, and out of the way.

Two surfaces: a popover for what is happening now, and a window when you want to look at the queue.

The interface is in Korean today. English and other languages are planned.

04 / Limits

What Reel does not do

A downloader that promises everything is usually lying about something. This is the honest list.

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to, no sync.
  • No telemetry. No analytics, no crash reports, no ads. The only network call Reel makes on its own is the update check.
  • No DRM circumvention. If a service encrypts the stream, Reel cannot take it. This is a downloader, not a key.
  • No browser extension. Copy from any browser instead — that is the whole interface.
  • No invented numbers. “1,800+ sites” is yt-dlp's extractor list, not a marketing figure. Some of those sites will be broken on any given day.

All extraction is yt-dlp's work, under the Unlicense. Reel is a thin, native front end.

05 / Install

Up and running in a minute.

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel. The app bundles its own yt-dlp and ffmpeg, so there is nothing else to install.

Download the zip, unzip it, and move Reel.app to your Applications folder.

Download Reel.app.zip
NOTE Reel is not notarized yet. On first launch macOS will refuse to open it: right-click Reel.app → Open, once. After that it launches normally. The Homebrew route skips this prompt.

Homebrew installs the same app and removes the quarantine prompt.

$ brew install --cask waylake/tap/reel
TRUST Homebrew 6.0 requires explicit trust for third-party taps. A fully qualified cask name trusts only this cask, not the whole tap.

Build the app yourself, with or without the bundled engine.

$ brew install xcodegen yt-dlp ffmpeg
$ git clone https://github.com/waylake/reel.git
$ cd reel && xcodegen generate
$ open Reel.xcodeproj

Contributions are welcome. Read CONTRIBUTING.md first.

NEEDS Xcode 16 or later, XcodeGen, and Homebrew. No Apple developer account is required for a local build.
06 / Questions

Answers, without the sales pitch.

Everything below is also in the docs, with the exact error messages.

Is Reel free?

Yes. MIT licensed, no paid tier, no ads, no upsell. The source is on GitHub.

A download failed with 403, or “the page needs to be reloaded”.

YouTube changed something. Open Settings → Advanced → yt-dlp → Update. The new engine takes effect immediately, without restarting the app.

Reel already retries transient errors with backoff and updates yt-dlp when a run fails, but a manual update is the fastest path.

macOS says the app cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer.

Reel is signed ad-hoc, not notarized by Apple. Right-click Reel.app in Applications and choose Open — once. Or install with Homebrew, which avoids the quarantine prompt entirely.

Does Reel upload anything about me?

No. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no server of ours. The clipboard is read locally to find links. Browser cookies, if you enable them, go only to the site you are downloading from. The app's own network calls are the update check and the download itself.

Can it download 4K?

Yes, when the source offers it. The Best quality MP4 preset takes the highest available video and audio and merges them with ffmpeg. 1080p H.264 is there for editors and older players that choke on VP9 or AV1.

Is downloading videos legal?

It depends on the content and where you live. Download what you own, what is offered under a licence that allows it, or what your local law permits. Respect each site's terms. Reel does not bypass DRM and does not host or distribute any media.

Where do my files go?

~/Movies/Reel by default. Change it in Settings → General. The queue lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Reel/queue.json.

Stop pasting URLs into boxes.
